What else changes with the tab bar:

  • iOS: haptic feedback on tab switch (UISelectionFeedbackGenerator)
  • iOS: translucent blur with vibrancy (BlurView underneath, no solid bg)
  • Android: ripple effect on touch (no scale/translate animation)
  • Android: filled icon variant for active, outlined for inactive
  • Both: tab bar respects system safe areas natively (no hardcoded paddingBottom: 25)
