Typography: How far native?

Currently the app uses Nunito (body) and Teko (display/scores). System fonts are SF Pro on iOS and Roboto on Android — both are already on-device, no download needed.

A

Full system font — replace everything

Nunito and Teko are removed. All text uses the platform system font (SF Pro / Roboto). The CText component switches to fontFamily: undefined per platform.
