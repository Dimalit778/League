Light-mode glass surfaces

A focused review of Champo’s My Leagues screen. The goal is to remove the dirty grey band from light cards and glass buttons while preserving the premium gold hierarchy and the existing dark appearance.

Executive synthesis

Observed fact

The defect is tonal, not structural

The supplied screen has clear hierarchy and readable content, but every highlight fades through a dark grey midpoint. The repeated band makes cards and buttons look stained.

Inference

Transparent black is being interpolated

The implementation fades a white RGBA highlight to the named color transparent. That endpoint is transparent black, so interpolation creates grey before opacity reaches zero.

Recommendation

Use a quiet surface in light mode

Remove the decorative sheen from light content cards and glass actions. Retain a subtle border and low elevation; reserve the warm tint for the primary league and keep the richer material treatment in dark mode.

Input analysis

Observed fact

The screenshot shows the My Leagues header, two glass actions, a premium primary-league card with a nested stats card and glass action, other league rows, and a Pro upsell card. The glass treatment repeats at three hierarchy levels.

What established systems do differently

Observed fact

Apple, Microsoft Fluent, Atlassian, and W3C are included as platform/design-system authorities, not because of app-store rankings. They document system-level surface, elevation, and accessibility behavior.

Inference

The common differentiator is semantic restraint: material choice communicates layer and function. Decorative gradients are subordinate to readable content and consistent elevation.

Fast, low-friction first run

Observed fact This is a returning-user dashboard, not onboarding. The earliest value is recognizing the primary league and entering it. The current screen requires no permission or setup decision before that action.

Recommendation Preserve the existing order, labels, defaults, locked-state disclosure, and direct “Enter league” action. The visual fix should not add steps, modals, sample data, or new decisions. Recovery behavior and account timing are outside this component’s scope.

Risks and anti-patterns

Observed fact

Accidental dark interpolation

A named transparent endpoint can introduce an unintended black hue into a light highlight.

Recommendation

Avoid nested spectacle

Do not strengthen blur, gloss, and shadow simultaneously across parent card, child card, and button.

Recommendation

Verify contrast

Recheck muted labels and disabled league rows against their final composited surfaces in both modes.

Inference

Android parity

Blur and elevation can differ by platform; the hue-preserving alpha fix is lower risk than increasing blur intensity.

Recommendations

P0 · High confidence · Low effort

Remove light-mode sheen

Render the highlight only in dark mode. Use a nearly opaque neutral gradient for regular cards and a very pale warm gradient for the primary card.

P1 · High confidence · Low effort

Use low, consistent elevation

Pair the light surface with a hairline border and a restrained cool-grey shadow. Glass actions should use the same surface language at a lower elevation.

P2 · Medium confidence · Medium effort

Composite contrast audit

Measure the final text/icon colors over the premium and neutral gradients, including locked/disabled states.

Proposed experience and implementation

Recommendation

State inventory

Default glass card, primary warm card, nested neutral card, glass action, pressed state, disabled/locked state, light mode, and dark mode.

Recommendation

Component rule

In light mode, content cards and actions use opaque surfaces without a sheen. In dark mode, a highlight may fade only opacity, never hue; the shared alpha utility keeps that interpolation safe.

Recommendation

Accessibility

Keep semantic buttons and labels unchanged. Test light/dark contrast, Reduce Transparency behavior where supported, RTL layout, and Dynamic Type clipping.

Recommendation

Measurement

No analytics event is warranted for a rendering fix. Validate with visual regression snapshots and monitor existing enter-league conversion only for unexpected regressions.
