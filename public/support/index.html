Champo Support

Account help, privacy, accessibility, and user safety

Email support@champoapp.com.

Report objectionable content or abusive users

In Champo, open the relevant member or league screen to report a nickname, profile image, or league name. You can also block a user immediately. League owners can remove members from their league.

Reports enter a private moderation queue. We aim to acknowledge safety reports within 24 hours and review them within 72 hours. Credible threats or urgent safety concerns are prioritized.
