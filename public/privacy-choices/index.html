Privacy Choices

Control your Champo account and personal information.

Access, correct, or export information

You can update supported profile information directly in the app. For a copy, correction, or export of other personal information, email support@champoapp.com from the address connected to your account. We may ask you to verify your identity before fulfilling the request.

Delete your account

Open Champo → Settings → Delete Account. The app explains what is deleted and what de-identified league history remains before you confirm. Account deletion cannot be undone.

Notifications

Match reminders are local device notifications. Disable them in Champo Settings or your device settings. Champo 1.0 has no purchases or subscriptions.

Contact

Email support@champoapp.com. Include the type of request and the email associated with your Champo account. Do not send passwords or payment card details.
